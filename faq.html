Soft washing

What is soft washing?

Soft washing cleans with low water pressure, under 500 PSI, and a biodegradable cleaning solution that kills algae, mildew and bacteria at the root. A pressure washer runs at 3,000 PSI or more and only blasts the surface. Soft washing gets stucco, siding and roofs clean without etching, stripping paint or forcing water behind walls.

Is soft washing safe for stucco?

Yes. Stucco is porous and easy to etch, which is why I never use high pressure on it. The solution does the cleaning and a low-pressure rinse carries it away. Cracked or loose stucco stays as it is; I point out anything I notice before I start.

Will soft washing hurt my plants, lawn or pets?

I wet down plants and lawn before I start, keep the solution off them as much as possible, and rinse them again when I'm done. Please keep pets inside until surfaces are rinsed and dry. Fish ponds and vegetable beds get covered.

How long does a clean last?

Because soft washing kills growth instead of just knocking it off, a house usually stays clean noticeably longer than after pressure washing. In the Valley, most homeowners book a house wash every one to two years and a roof wash every few years.

Solar panel cleaning

How often should solar panels be cleaned in Bakersfield and the Central Valley?

For most Kern County homes, two to four times a year. Field and road dust, almond-bloom pollen in late winter, harvest dust in the fall and bird droppings all settle on the glass. Homes near orchards, dirt roads or freeways usually need it more often.

Do dirty solar panels really produce less power?

Yes. Anything that blocks sunlight reduces how much power a panel makes, and Valley dust builds up quickly between rains. The simplest check is your inverter or monitoring app: compare a sunny day before and after a cleaning.

Will cleaning void my solar panel warranty?

Most panel makers warn against pressure washers, abrasive pads and harsh chemicals. I use a soft-bristle brush and purified water only, which is the gentle method manufacturers generally recommend. If you have your warranty papers handy, I'm happy to look at the cleaning section with you.

Why not just hose the panels off myself?

Tap water in the Valley is hard and leaves mineral spots as it dries, and a hose alone won't lift baked-on dust or droppings. Climbing onto a roof is also the riskiest part of the job. I bring purified water, a soft brush and the right ladder setup.

When is the best time of year to clean solar panels?

Late spring, after the almond bloom and before the long summer production months, is the most valuable cleaning of the year. A second cleaning in the fall after harvest dust settles is a good habit too.

How much does soft washing or solar panel cleaning cost?

Every home is different, so I give a free written quote before any work starts. The price depends on the size of the house, how many stories, how many panels and how dirty everything is. The number you get in writing is the number you pay.

I'm based in Bakersfield and serve the valley floor of Kern County: Bakersfield, Oildale, Rosedale, Shafter, Wasco, Delano, McFarland, Arvin, Lamont, Taft, Buttonwillow and Maricopa. If you're nearby in the southern San Joaquin Valley, ask and I'll tell you straight whether I can get there.

Are you insured?

Yes, Rinse & Restore is fully insured. I can send proof of insurance with your quote.

Do I need to be home?

Not always. I need access to an outdoor water spigot and any side gates, and pets kept inside. Many customers are home for the start and the final walk-through, but we can set it up so you don't have to be.

How long does a job take?

Most single-story house washes and typical rooftop solar arrays are done in a few hours. Larger or two-story homes and roof washes can take most of a day. Your quote includes a time estimate.

What happens if it rains or it's too windy?

I'll text you to reschedule at no charge. Wind affects soft washing, so on very windy days I'll move the job to protect your plants, cars and neighbors.
