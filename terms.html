Existing conditions and results

Soft washing removes dirt, algae, mildew and most organic staining. Some stains, such as rust, hard-water deposits, oxidation, paint overspray and old oil, may lighten but not fully come out. I'll tell you before I start if I expect that.

Cleaning can reveal problems that were already there and hidden under dirt, such as faded or failing paint, cracked stucco, worn shingles or panel damage. I'm not responsible for those pre-existing conditions.

Solar panels are cleaned with a soft brush and purified water only. I don't open, adjust or repair any part of a solar system or its wiring.
